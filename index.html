Featured Scholarship

The Chris Lyons First Responder Scholarship

Photo — Chris Lyons or a past recipient

Established in honor of Chris Lyons, this scholarship celebrates his more than 30 years of dedicated service to Catoosa County and the example he has set as a devoted father. Inspired by his commitment to both family and community, the scholarship supports the next generation of first responder families by providing educational opportunities for their children.

Award
$1,000
Open To
Heritage, Ringgold & LFO Seniors
Deadline
March 15, 2027
Apply Now → Support the Scholarship →

Scholarship Details

Who Can Apply

Applicants must:

  • Be a graduating high school senior at Heritage, Ringgold, or LFO
  • Have a parent or guardian who is an active or retired first responder (law enforcement, firefighter, EMT, or paramedic)
  • Plan to attend a college, university, or trade school after graduation

What to Submit

  • Completed scholarship application form
  • A 300–500 word essay answering:
“How has your parent’s service as a first responder influenced your life and shaped the person you hope to become?”

How It’s Judged

Applications are reviewed based on:

  • Character
  • Community involvement
  • Commitment to future goals
  • Essay response

How to Apply

  1. Download & Complete

    Print or download the application below, fill it out, and write your 300–500 word essay.

  2. Submit by March 15

    Turn in your application and essay to your school counseling office by March 15, 2027.

  3. Award Announced

    The recipient will be announced at the Senior Awards Ceremony in May 2027.

Scholarship Application

Download and complete the application below, then turn it in with your essay to your school counseling office by March 15, 2027.

Common Questions

Who is eligible?

Graduating seniors at Heritage, Ringgold, or LFO High School whose parent or guardian is an active or retired first responder — law enforcement, firefighter, EMT, or paramedic — and who plan to attend a college, university, or trade school after graduation.

Where do I turn in my application?

Submit your completed application and essay to your high school’s counseling office by March 15, 2027.

How long should the essay be?

300–500 words, answering: “How has your parent’s service as a first responder influenced your life and shaped the person you hope to become?”

How is the award paid?

The $1,000 is issued directly to the recipient’s college, university, or trade school upon confirmation of enrollment.

When is the recipient announced?

At the Senior Awards Ceremony in May 2027.

Shelter Adoptions

Helping Local Pets Find Home

The Lyons Society helps support local animals by paying adoption fees for pets at local animal shelters — giving every pet a better chance at finding a permanent home and helping reduce the number of abandoned animals in our community.

  2. We Cover the Fee

    More pets leave the shelter for permanent homes, and fewer animals are abandoned.
